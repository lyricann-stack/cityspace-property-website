For Tenants

Everything you need to know about repairs and rent payments.

Repair request process

  • Submit a request

    Let us know the issue and your property address by email or phone.

  • Assessment

    We confirm the issue and arrange a qualified tradesperson.

  • On-site work

    The tradesperson attends; urgent issues are prioritised.

  • Follow-up

    We confirm the work is complete and close out the request.
